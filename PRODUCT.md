# Product

## Register

product

## Users

B3S is used by brand strategists, founders, evaluators, and internal operators who need to inspect how a brand is being scored from captured evidence. Their working context is investigative: they compare scans, inspect citations, diagnose missing evidence, review visual acquisition, and decide whether a score is justified.

The primary unit is the brand. Runs, scanners, acquisition steps, tiles, and reports exist to explain the brand state, not to become the mental model themselves.

## Product Purpose

B3S is a brand evidence lab. It captures web, search, repository, external mention, and visual evidence; classifies that evidence into the SV9 model; and renders reports where every number can be traced back to acquisition records, cited snippets, absent evidence, or visible limitations.

Success means a user can answer: what did the system find, what did it miss, why did the score change, which evidence supports each component, and whether a scan is reliable enough to compare with another one.

## Brand Personality

Technical, analytical, and severe. The product should feel like an expert instrument: dense, controlled, precise, and skeptical of its own output. It should preserve the legacy B3S terminal character while making reports easier to read and compare.

The voice is direct and evidentiary. It avoids motivational language, marketing gloss, and explanations that cannot be backed by captured records.

## Anti-references

Do not make B3S look like a generic SaaS dashboard, CRM, investor analytics panel, or AI startup landing page.

Avoid beige editorial Brand3 styling unless explicitly reviving a historical screen. The current product direction is darker RGB terminal, mono-forward, evidence-first, and operational.

Avoid purple gradients, glossy AI surfaces, yellow warning semantics, decorative cards, nested cards, ornamental charts, hero marketing layouts, and rows of equally weighted information that hide the brand and score.

Avoid hiding acquisition limitations. Degraded evidence, blocked visual capture, failed providers, and fallback decisions must remain visible and countable.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before opinion. Every score, absence, warning, and recommendation must be reachable from citations, artifacts, or acquisition state.
2. Brand first. The brand name, domain, visual evidence, current score, and immediate gaps should be visible before internal run metadata.
3. Controlled density. B3S should hold a lot of information, but the first view should reduce noise and push details into drawers, evidence panels, or secondary views.
4. Technical composition. Layout should feel like an observatory or calibration desk: grids, panels, terminal rhythm, clear hierarchy, and no decorative softness.
5. Comparable scans. The interface must make differences between local and remote runs, acquisition quality, evidence coverage, and component outcomes legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls. Do not encode status only by color; pair color with text labels such as warning, blocked, detected, absent, or insufficient acquisition.

Respect reduced motion. Animations may indicate loading or state change, but content must remain visible without animation. Scrollable drawers should work without visible scrollbar chrome when appropriate, but keyboard focus and dialog dismissal must remain standard.
